### What gets fixed in your pitch deck financials

Four areas come up in almost every deck.

**How the numbers are presented.** Figures that are correct but hard to read, charts that hide the point, and totals that don't match from one slide to the next.

**Projections.** Forecasts that don't follow from your current numbers, or growth assumptions nobody could defend in a meeting.

**Market research.** Market size figures that need a clear source and a clear line to the revenue you're projecting.

**Where the company sits in its market.** Positioning claims backed by numbers, so the comparison with competitors holds up.

Unit economics and chart clean-up are part of every review.
